# PROMPT — Les enfants Manodrey (silhouettes)

Régénération de la série de silhouettes d'enfants du dossier `assets` (children-walking, girls-pair, girl-up, reach). Style : silhouette noire pleine, découpe vectorielle, fond blanc, aucun détail intérieur.

Format conseillé : 16:9 pour la rangée de cinq. Pour un détourage propre, générer plutôt deux images de deux ou trois figures : meilleure résolution par personnage.

---

## PROMPT PRINCIPAL (version améliorée)

```
A professional silhouette illustration sheet showing five separate children figures,
arranged in one evenly spaced horizontal row on a pure white background, each figure fully
isolated with generous empty space around it, no overlapping, no shared ground line, no
cast shadows, no baseline.

STYLE LOCK, identical on all five figures: solid pure black filled silhouette, flat matte
black, no gradient, no shading, no outline, no interior detail whatsoever, no visible face,
no facial features, no hair strands, no clothing folds, no color. Crisp clean vector-style
edges with smooth confident curves, high contrast cut-out treatment, the quality of a
hand-cut paper silhouette.

SILHOUETTE READABILITY RULES, apply to every figure: the pose must read instantly from the
outline alone. Keep clear negative space between the arms and the torso, never let a limb
merge into the body mass, separate the legs so the gap between them is visible, keep the
chin and neck line distinct from the shoulders, and let hair, hat brim and clothing hem
break the outline with a recognizable shape. Every hand and every foot is a readable
shape, never a blunt stump.

CHILD PROPORTIONS, essential: these are children, not small adults. Large head relative to
the body, short limbs, soft rounded torso, high waist, short neck. Roughly five and a half
head-heights for the youngest, six and a half for the oldest. Slightly bowed relaxed legs
and small rounded hands and feet.

FIGURE 1, the walk. A girl of about eight walking in profile toward the right, holding the
hand of a smaller boy of about four who follows a step behind. She wears a flared
knee-length dress whose hem swings back with the movement. Both are caught mid-stride with
legs clearly apart, her free arm swinging forward, his trailing arm stretched back by their
joined hands. Line of action: a forward diagonal, energetic, going somewhere.

FIGURE 2, the pact. Two children standing face to face, holding both hands, seen from the
side. A taller girl of about ten, long ponytail falling clear of her shoulder, long-sleeved
top and shorts, and a smaller boy of about six, short hair, t-shirt and shorts, both
barefoot, weight settled on both feet. The gap between their two bodies forms a clean
symmetrical shape. Line of action: two facing verticals, still, complicit.

FIGURE 3, the sky. A small girl of about five standing in three-quarter profile, head
tilted far back so the throat line is fully exposed and the chin points upward. She wears a
simple buttonless coat and small boots, and holds a large round wide-brimmed hat at her
side, arm relaxed, the hat brim forming a strong circular shape against her body. Line of
action: a soft backward curve from the heels to the crown of the head. Wonder.

FIGURE 4, the reach. A child of about seven standing on tiptoe, one arm stretched straight
up above the head, fingers open and separated, reaching for something high out of frame,
the other arm hanging loose along the body, the torso arched slightly backward and the heels
lifted off the ground. Line of action: one long vertical stretch from the toes to the
fingertips. Desire.

FIGURE 5, the wait. A child of about six sitting cross-legged on the ground, back straight,
head tilted up, both hands resting palms down on the knees, seen in profile. The crossed
legs form a wide stable triangular base. Line of action: a calm pyramid. Patience.

All five figures are full body head to toe, feet fully visible, nothing cropped, scaled
correctly relative to each other by age, rendered with the identical flat black treatment.
Clean editorial silhouette sheet, print quality, razor sharp edges, 4K.

Negative: no text, no watermark, no logos, no numbers, no captions, no frame borders, no
grey tones, no gradients, no shading, no outlines, no line art, no hollow figures, no
faces, no facial features, no eyes, no color, no background objects, no furniture, no props
except the hat, no ground shadow, no floor line, no overlapping figures, no more than five
figures, no adults, no adult body proportions, no cropped limbs, no merged limbs, no
deformed hands or feet, pure white background only.
```

Les trois blocs qui font la qualité du rendu : les proportions par âge (sinon on obtient des adultes rétrécis), les règles de lisibilité (un bras collé au torse détruit la silhouette), et la ligne d'action qui donne une intention à chaque pose.
